1. Subject of the Contract
The subject of the project is the conception, development, implementation, and deployment of a custom digital software solution for the client.
The solution consists of:
a) a web-based application for administration, scheduling, project execution, and office organization, and
b) a mobile application for field staff on iOS and Android.
The software is intended to digitally map, support, and manage operational business processes, particularly in the areas of:
CRM
Asset management
Project management
Scheduling
Service reports
Quotation management
Order processing
Document management
Material management
Task management
User management
Data migration from existing systems
Unless otherwise agreed, the contractor owes a fully functional solution according to this specification, the technical requirements, defined interfaces, and acceptance criteria.
